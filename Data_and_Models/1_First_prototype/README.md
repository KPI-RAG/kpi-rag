# First prototype (Phase 1, September 2026)

The notebooks behind the first version of the models, in the order they were run:
data loading and exploration (01), feature engineering and split analysis (02, 02b),
the feature and algorithm experiments (03 to 03e), the session-level split (04), and the
data and modeling pipelines that produced the Phase 1 models (05, 06).

Figures and result files written by the notebooks are in `notebooks/data/`.

Notebooks 03, 03b and 03d were saved without their outputs; their results are reported in the Phase 1 report.
